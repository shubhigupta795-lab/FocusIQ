Compare clustering quality

Silhouette ranges from −1 to 1; higher indicates better separation in the standardized feature space. Values near zero suggest substantial overlap. The PCA calculation is retained as a dimensionality-reduction demonstration, but the profiles above use original hours.

Model selection & optimization

Three classes: Low (below 40), Normal (40 to below 70), High (70–100). These are project-defined thresholds. Three-fold grouped cross-validation selects the highest macro F1 on training data; the same held-out students evaluate every model.

Inspect any trained classifier

Precision, recall and F1 are macro averages (each class has equal weight). ROC AUC is the macro one-vs-rest average. The dropdown is for comparison only; it does not change the deployed model.
